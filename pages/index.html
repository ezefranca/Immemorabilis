Features

Works with Reminders.

No import or export. Apple Reminders stays the source of truth.

↻

Notifications repeat

Missed notifications return at the interval you choose.

◷

Quick snooze

Move a task by 15 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, or until tomorrow.

Aa

Natural dates

Type a date or time in the task title.

▦

Widgets

View, add, or dictate without opening the app.

Product principles

Some ground rules.

01

It is reminder software.

It does not diagnose ADHD or any other condition.

02

Mental health is not ad copy.

No shame, implied diagnosis, or claims about what is wrong with a person.

03

Pricing should match the product.

Reminders underneath does not turn every interface into gold. Read the room.

04

Academic work is not the only work.

Use it for a paper, a lecture, a passport renewal, or groceries.
